How to use / how it works

Table / Discard: tap every card you see come out for other players (and anything discarded). My Hand: tap the cards dealt to you. The deck isn't reshuffled between rounds in Flip 7, so cards keep counting until the draw pile runs out.

End round moves your hand to the discard pile (still out of the deck). Reshuffle deck when the draw pile runs out — it puts all seen cards back, keeping only what's currently in your hand out. New game resets everything.

If you add a number you already have, the app treats it as a bust — or, if you hold a Second Chance, uses it automatically.

Recommendation comes from an optimal look-ahead: it computes the expected score of hitting and then continuing to play perfectly (stop when standing is worth more) against the exact remaining deck, including x2, +modifiers, Second Chance and the +15 Flip 7 bonus. Freeze and Flip Three are assumed to be given to an opponent. It maximizes your expected round score only — if you're far behind near 200, lean toward hitting; if you're about to win, lean toward standing.
