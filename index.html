Discard pile
How to use / how it works

Setup: enter the players in seating (turn) order and pick which one is you.

Dealing: the bar above the cards shows who gets the next card. Tap the card that's dealt and it goes to that player, then the turn moves to the next player still in the round. Tap Stay when the current player stays. If the order goes wrong, tap a player's name → Give them the next card.

Action cards: tap Action when one is dealt and choose what it was: Flip Four / Just One More send the next 4 / 1 cards to the target, then play returns to the normal order. Freeze makes the target stay. Swap, Steal and Discard ask you to tap the cards involved.

Fixing things: tap any card on the table to move it to another player, swap it with another card, discard it, or remove it (if you tapped the wrong card). Undo works for everything.

Special cards are applied automatically: Unlucky 7 discards that player's other number and modifier cards; Lucky 13 allows a second 13 (a third busts); a duplicate number busts; 7 number cards is a Flip 7 (+15) and ends the round.

End round adds round scores to the totals and moves all cards to the discard pile, which stays out of the deck until you Reshuffle deck (when the draw pile runs out). The next round starts with the next player.

Scoring used: add number cards, apply ÷2 (rounded down), subtract the negatives, never below 0. Holding the 0 scores 0 unless you Flip 7, and you must keep hitting.

Recommendation is for you only: the expected score of hitting and then playing perfectly against the exact remaining deck (looking up to 6 cards ahead). It assumes action cards are played on opponents and maximizes this round's score. If you're far behind near 200, lean toward hitting; if you're about to win, lean toward standing.
