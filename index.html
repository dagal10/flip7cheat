How to use / how it works

Built for Flip 7: With a Vengeance (108 cards): one each of 0 and 1, then n copies of each number up to thirteen 13s (one 7 is the Unlucky 7, one 13 is the Lucky 13), one each of −2, −4, −6, −8, −10 and ÷2, and 10 action cards.

Table / Discard: tap every card you see come out for other players (and anything discarded). They're listed under On the table in the order you tapped them, so you can check what's out if the deal goes fast. My Hand: tap the cards in front of you.

End round moves the table and your hand to the Discard pile (still out of the deck). Reshuffle deck when the draw pile runs out — it puts the discard pile back, but cards still on the table and in your hand stay out. Cards stay out of the deck between rounds until you reshuffle.

If a Swap, Steal or Discard changes your hand, tap a chip in "My hand" to remove a card, or add the new one in My Hand mode. Removing a chip puts that card back in the deck; if it went to the discard pile, also tap it in Table mode.

In My Hand mode the app applies the special cards for you. Unlucky 7 discards your other number and modifier cards. Lucky 13 lets you hold a second 13; a third 13 busts. A duplicate number busts.

Scoring used: add your number cards, apply ÷2 (rounded down), subtract the negatives, never below 0. Seven number cards = Flip 7, +15. Holding the 0 makes your round score 0 unless you Flip 7, and you must keep hitting.

Recommendation comes from an optimal look-ahead: the expected score of hitting and then playing perfectly against the exact remaining deck. Action cards are assumed to be played on opponents. It maximizes your expected round score only. If you're far behind near 200, lean toward hitting; if you're about to win, lean toward standing.
